# ITEDA Solutions

**Solar-powered equipment and simple digital tools for farmers and small businesses in Kenya.**

We build machines that run on the sun, the software that monitors them, and the services that put them to work. Founded in 2021, grown at Strathmore University, and based on Five Star Road, Nairobi.

## Why we exist

Most harvests in Kenya are still dried on the ground, under open sky. When it rains at harvest, the crop stays wet and spoils in store. About a sixth of Kenya's maize harvest is lost after harvest, much of it during drying ([APHLIS, 2021](https://www.aphlis.net/en/data/tables/overview/KE/maize/2021)).

**Mission:** We build solar-powered equipment and simple digital tools that help farmers and small businesses earn more from their work and cope with a changing climate.

**Vision:** Farms and small businesses running on clean, reliable infrastructure they can afford.

## What we make

### Smart Solar Crop Dryer
A solar-powered drying chamber with sensors and a controller. It keeps temperature and humidity in range while the crop dries, and sends every reading to our monitoring platform, so each batch has a record. We design and build it ourselves, now on its third version, with a fourth in design.
[itedasolutions.com/crop-dryer](https://itedasolutions.com/crop-dryer)

### Drying as a Service
Farmers and cooperatives book a dryer for their harvest instead of buying one. We set it up near where the crop is stored, run it, and charge per kilogram for the moisture we remove.
[itedasolutions.com/drying-service](https://itedasolutions.com/drying-service)

### bridGe
A Google Forms add-on that collects M-Pesa payments. When someone submits a form, bridGe sends an M-Pesa prompt to their phone for the total, the money goes to the organiser's own Paybill, Till or bank account, and the response sheet shows who has paid.
[bridge.itedasolutions.com](https://bridge.itedasolutions.com)

## What we build here

Most of our repositories are private, because they hold product code and device firmware. They cover:

- **Dryer firmware:** the controller that runs each dryer and reports its readings.
- **Monitoring platform:** the web app and API where we and our customers follow every dryer and every batch ([app.itedasolutions.com](https://app.itedasolutions.com)).
- **bridGe:** the add-on and the payment backend behind it.
- **Websites and infrastructure:** our public sites and the server they run on.
